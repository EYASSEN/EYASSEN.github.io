Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to Docs to MD 1.1.0 and later · a Yassin Labs tool, published by Yassin Abd El Aal

Yassin Labs · Docs to MD

Docs to MD does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It works entirely on your own computer and never connects to the internet.

What the app does with your files

You choose a folder. The app reads the documents in it (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images and text files) and writes Markdown copies into a new folder next to it. Reading, text recognition (OCR) and conversion all run locally on your computer. Your documents and the converted files never leave your computer and are never sent to the publisher or to anyone else.

Information the app keeps

  • Your conversion settings (language, quality and similar options), saved on your computer so they are there next time. In the Microsoft Store version they are kept in your Windows user profile; uninstalling the app removes them.
  • A conversion report and temporary working files on your computer, used to resume or skip finished conversions. They stay on your computer.

None of this is sent anywhere.

No network, analytics or advertising

  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no advertising.
  • It sends no analytics, crash reports or usage data. Telemetry in the bundled ONNX Runtime component is turned off.
  • The bundled components (Tesseract OCR, PaddleOCR models run by ONNX Runtime, Java for older Office files, PyMuPDF and others) run on your computer only and do not download anything while the app runs.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For questions, contact the publisher through the support link on the app's Microsoft Store page.
