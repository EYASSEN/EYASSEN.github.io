Docs to MD

Version 1.1.0 · Windows 10 and 11 (x64) · free

Convert whole folders of PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and scanned documents to Markdown, on your own computer, with Arabic and English OCR. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Get it

  • Microsoft Store — the easiest way; installs and updates like any Store app.

What it reads

  • PDF, including scanned pages, mixed pages and tables
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint, including old .doc, .xls and .ppt files
  • Images (PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP, WEBP), CSV, TSV, HTML, text and Markdown

Every run ends with a report of what converted fully, what converted with warnings, and what could not be read. OCR is an approximate reading: check numbers from scanned pages against the page image the app keeps beside the text.

Privacy

The app works offline and collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
